# Water textures

`deep.png`, `mid.png` and `white.png` are 64x64 seamless cuts from the water texture sheet Traver supplied on 2026-11-03 (generated by Traver, likely with PixelLab). They are starting tiles: replace them with Traver's own seamless PixelLab tiles by dropping new PNGs over these files, or by dragging new textures into `data/looks/water_spring.tres`.
