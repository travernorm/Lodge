# Bank textures

`cut_bank.png` (64x16) is the cut bank face: a grass lip on top, crumbly topsoil, earth, and dark mud at the waterline. Traver made it in PixelLab on 2026-11-05 ("Cliff one.aseprite", tile 12 of 16, 64x64). It was shrunk to 64x16 layer by layer (border removed; grass 9 rows to 3, topsoil 4 to 2, earth 40 to 9, mud 3 to 2), keeping the tile's own 4 colours, because a 1 m bank face is about 8 to 16 px tall at 8 px per metre. It repeats left to right. Replace it by dropping a new PNG over this file, or by dragging another texture into `data/bank_looks/spring.tres`.

The wet stones, dry gravel and sand slots in the look are empty: placeholder pebbles stand in until Traver's textures arrive (64x64, seamless).
